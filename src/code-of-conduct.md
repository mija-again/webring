# Code of Conduct

Spankscuary is a hand-built neighbourhood of independent websites connected by choice. The point is to help readers find spanking stories, art, history, humour, blogs, archives, forums, and other community resources made and maintained by actual people.

Membership is open to independently maintained sites with substantial spanking-related content. Sites do not all need to look alike, sound alike, or have the same interests. In fact, that would rather defeat the purpose.

Spankscuary is built around a simple expectation of good neighbourliness. Don't harass people, deliberately send trouble toward another member site, impersonate other people, publish private information without permission, or use the ring to distribute material you do not have the right to share.

Member sites also need to remain connected to the ring. If your site is listed in Spankscuary, please display the Spankscuary navigation banner or another approved link back into the ring somewhere readers can reasonably find it.

Otherwise, don't make our ring a horseshoe.

Sites get redesigned. Widgets break. Hosting companies have opinions. Nobody is going to dispatch the Webring Police because a banner disappears for an afternoon. But if a member site stops linking back to Spankscuary altogether, we'll get in touch and try to sort it out. A webring only works when readers can keep travelling around it.

Spankscuary is not an archive that gathers other people's work. It connects people to work its makers have chosen to share on their own sites. Members remain responsible for their own websites, their own writing and artwork, and their own decisions about what they publish.

If you're unsure whether your site belongs here, or something in the ring isn't working, [email Mija](https://mijasroom.net/contact/). I'm currently tending Spankscuary and adding new member sites.
